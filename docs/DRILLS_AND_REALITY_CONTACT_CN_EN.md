# Drills and Reality Contact / 演练与现实接触

A checklist creates confidence; a drill creates evidence.

## Required drills

### Communication outage

Simulate the primary phone/data channel being unavailable. Household members use the printed plan, out-of-area contact and meeting points. Record time to contact, missing numbers and failed assumptions.

### Identity lockout

Using synthetic data only, rehearse how to recover email, mobile carrier and a critical account from a clean device. Never expose real recovery codes during a group exercise.

### Payment outage

Simulate the primary card or wallet being unavailable for 24 hours. Confirm alternative lawful payment and essential-bill contacts without making unnecessary real transactions.

### Deepfake emergency

A trusted person sends a synthetic urgent request. The recipient must stop, independently call back, use the private challenge protocol and involve a second person.

### Medication or care interruption

Rehearse who holds the current medication list, which clinician or pharmacist is contacted, and who can provide dependent care. Do not alter medication for the drill.

### Temporary relocation

Pack a go-bag, test two routes and confirm a destination. The exercise must not endanger participants or enter restricted property.

## Reality-contact record

For each drill record:

```text
prediction
actual time
what failed
who was burdened
what was missing
what caused confusion
what will change
owner
deadline
retest date
```

A model or checklist that repeatedly fails should be revised or retired. Completing a drill is not success if the household could not actually communicate, recover access or protect dependents.
