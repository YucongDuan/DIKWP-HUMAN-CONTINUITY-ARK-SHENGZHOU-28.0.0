# Localization Guide / 本地化指南

The core client is jurisdiction-neutral. Before institutional deployment, create a Local Continuity Pack containing:

- official emergency alert channels;
- emergency-service and non-emergency numbers;
- disaster and evacuation guidance;
- public health and poison-control contacts;
- banking, identity and mobile-carrier recovery routes;
- social protection, unemployment and food-support access;
- local shelter, cooling/heating and transport resources;
- disability, language and accessibility support;
- worker, tenant and consumer rights contacts;
- verification date and responsible maintainer.

A Local Continuity Pack must distinguish official sources, service-provider statements, community resources and unverified suggestions. It must never copy one country's emergency numbers or legal assumptions into another jurisdiction without validation.
