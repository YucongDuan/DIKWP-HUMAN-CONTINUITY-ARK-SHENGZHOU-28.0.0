# Eight AGI-Era Discontinuity Worlds / 八个AGI时代失效世界

## 1. Rapid automation and income shock / 快速自动化与收入冲击

**Observable signals:** accelerated layoffs, entry-level task compression, abrupt contract conversion, loss of bargaining power, dependency on one platform or employer.

**Preparation:** 30-day essential-expense runway, two income routes, verifiable work evidence, one locally useful skill, official benefit and employment contacts.

## 2. AI-enabled fraud, impersonation and influence / AI欺诈、冒用与影响操纵

**Observable signals:** urgent voice/video requests, secrecy demands, payment instructions, cloned family voices, fake officials, mass persuasion and fabricated evidence.

**Preparation:** callback to known numbers, private challenge questions, second trusted person, no action from one channel, preserved evidence.

## 3. Cyber compromise and identity lockout / 网络入侵与身份锁死

**Observable signals:** unexpected password reset, SIM transfer, lost email access, new OAuth grants, altered recovery contacts, benefit or bank-account takeover.

**Preparation:** phishing-resistant MFA where available, offline recovery codes, clean-device path, protected document copies, official recovery contacts.

## 4. Cloud, payment and communications outage / 云、支付与通信中断

**Observable signals:** multi-provider outage, unavailable wallet, widespread login failure, mobile-network congestion, unavailable cloud contacts.

**Preparation:** two payment rails, modest lawful cash reserve, paper contacts, offline maps and sources, local radio, power banks.

## 5. Essential-service and supply disruption / 基本服务和供应中断

**Observable signals:** official utility warning, medication shortage, transport disruption, water or food distribution failure.

**Preparation:** several days of supplies, medication continuity planned with clinicians, utility shutoff knowledge, temperature backup, local mutual aid.

## 6. Autonomous-agent or system-control incident / 自主Agent或系统控制事故

**Observable signals:** actions outside declared purpose, unexplained tool calls, automated purchases, hidden delegation, account changes without confirmation.

**Preparation:** inventory every AI integration, least privilege, revoke unused grants, human confirmation for irreversible actions, independent activity logs.

## 7. Local safety failure and temporary relocation / 局部安全失效与临时迁移

**Observable signals:** evacuation notice, violence, infrastructure failure, unsafe housing, heat/cold emergency.

**Preparation:** two routes, two meeting points, destination plan, go-bag, medications, papers, transport backup and pet plan.

## 8. Compound discontinuity / 复合连续性危机

This world combines several failures. It is not a prediction of global collapse; it tests whether the household has hidden single points of failure. The goal is graceful degradation rather than perfect independence.
