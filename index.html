AGI DISCONTINUITY · CIVILIAN CONTINUITY · REALITY CONTACT

When models, platforms, payments, identity systems and public services change together, people still need to meet essential needs, verify facts, protect their families and recover.

SHENGZHOU organizes observable failures—income loss, impersonation fraud, account takeover, communication and payment outages, medicine or energy shortages, unauthorized automation, displacement and compound crises—into practical preparation, triggers, actions and review. It does not predict a date for loss of AGI control.

Keep the minimum summary and access instructions needed for emergency care and recovery. Do not store original diagnostic records in this application.

Name and emergency contact

FAMILY TRUST PROTOCOL

Communication, impersonation and emergency-request verification

A voice, video or familiar writing style is insufficient on its own to establish identity. Verify consequential requests through another trusted channel.

Check an urgent message

Three-step family verification

1. End the incoming contact

Do not pay, share verification codes or install software during an unverified call, message or video request.

2. Call back through a known channel

Use a previously saved or written number, not a new number supplied by the caller.

3. Private challenge and second-person check

Ask a nonpublic question and verify with another trusted person.

MONEY AND LIVELIHOOD

Payments, cash flow and 90-day income continuity

Assess access, redemption, identity, payment channels, income and essential living costs alongside the balance.

90-day income disruption plan

  1. Separate spending into essentials, responsibilities and deferrable items.
  2. Keep at least two income or mutual-aid routes with different failure exposures.
  3. Turn one capability into a bounded service supported by outside evidence.
  4. Avoid using leverage, training loans, speculation or fraudulent schemes to respond to panic.
  5. Review actual results on days 7, 30, 60 and 90.
DIGITAL SOVEREIGNTY

Accounts, identity, devices and offline backups

Prepare for loss of cloud access, the main phone or the primary email account. Keep recovery resources outside the affected system.

After an account takeover

1

Move to a trusted device
Do not continue changing passwords on a device suspected of compromise.

2

Secure the primary email account and mobile-carrier account
Other accounts often depend on these recovery channels.

3

Revoke sessions and third-party access
Disable AI agents, extensions and unfamiliar OAuth access.

4

Contact official channels and preserve evidence
Record times, transactions and notices; do not negotiate with an attacker.

MOBILITY AND MUTUAL AID

Relocation, meeting points and three-person mutual aid

Build a support network with different skills and locations while sharing only necessary information. Avoid dependence on either isolation or a single central point.

Three-person mutual-aid unit

Role A: local action — Local conditions, transport, neighbors and material resources.
Role B: information and communication — Official sources, paper contacts and out-of-area relay.
Role C: care and recovery — Children, older people, medication, emotional support and follow-up.

One person may hold several roles, but avoid placing all capabilities, devices and information with one person.

Contact register; omit sensitive identity information
