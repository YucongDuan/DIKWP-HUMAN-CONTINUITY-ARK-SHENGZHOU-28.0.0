# SHENGZHOU 28 — 90-Day Personal Adoption Plan / 90天个人实施计划

## Week 1 / 第1周
- Three trusted contacts, one out-of-area contact, two channels and two meeting points.
- 三名可信联系人、一名异地联系人、两个通道、两个会合点。
- Critical-account MFA, offline recovery codes and AI/agent inventory.
- 关键账户MFA、离线恢复码与AI/Agent权限清单。

## Weeks 2–4 / 第2–4周
- Medicine and care continuity, protected document copies, separated backups, alternate payment route.
- 药物与照护连续性、证件副本、分离备份与备用支付。
- Run a family deepfake drill and a communications-outage drill.
- 完成家庭深伪与通信中断演练。

## Month 2 / 第2个月
- Build a 30-day essential-expense plan and one second income or mutual-aid route.
- 建立30天必要开支计划及一条第二收入或互助路线。
- Test two relocation routes and a destination.
- 测试两条迁移路线和一个目的地。

## Month 3 / 第3个月
- Complete identity, payment, medicine/care and relocation drills.
- 完成身份、支付、药物/照护和迁移演练。
- Repair the weakest life floor, assign an owner and schedule a retest.
- 修复最弱底线，明确负责人并安排复测。
